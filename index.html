WEB APPLICATION GROUP PROJECT PBDE401 · PLATFORM BASED DEVELOPMENT

CodeCrafters — DUT Student Ticketing Portal

A web-based grievance ticketing system for Durban University of Technology students. Instead of complaints getting lost in emails and queues, a student submits an issue to the right department, receives a unique ticket number, and follows it through to resolution with a 72-hour response target. Built by Group 2 for my first-semester Platform Based Development (PBDE401) module.

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

  • Students register with their DUT email and student number, then sign in to a secure account.
  • Tickets are submitted to one of 10 departments, with a description, a priority level and supporting documents attached.
  • Every ticket gets a unique reference number and a live status: Pending, Assigned, In Progress or Resolved.
  • Admins review incoming tickets and assign them to the right staff member.
  • Staff accept tickets, add progress updates and mark them resolved; students are notified and can rate the service.

HOW A TICKET MOVES

  1. Student submits an issue and receives a reference number PENDING
  2. Admin reviews it and assigns it to a staff member ASSIGNED
  3. Staff work on it and post updates the student can follow IN PROGRESS
  4. Staff resolve it; the student is notified and rates the service RESOLVED

WEB APPLICATION COMMUNITY PROJECT

IT Graduates Mentorship Platform

A peer-mentorship web platform that connects working IT graduates (mentors) with graduates still looking for their first opportunity (mentees). Members sign up online, a coordinator pairs them by specialisation, and each pair gets the other's contact details to start talking on WhatsApp. It is a free, serverless site: the front end runs on GitHub Pages, and Firebase handles sign-in and data.

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

  • A sign-up form for mentors and mentees capturing name, WhatsApp number, specialisation, goals or skills to share, and availability, with a consent tick-box for sharing contact details.
  • Mentors set how many mentees they can take (1 to 5), so no mentor is overloaded.
  • A coordinator dashboard shows live totals (mentors, mentees, matched, waiting) and lets the coordinator match or unmatch each mentee.
  • Mentor suggestions are filtered by free capacity and starred when the specialisation matches the mentee's field.
  • Matched members see their partner's details and a one-tap WhatsApp chat button, updated in real time.

HOW A MATCH HAPPENS

  1. Join as a mentor or mentee and complete a profile
  2. Coordinator reviews everyone waiting to be matched
  3. Match by specialisation and mentor capacity
  4. Connect on WhatsApp and begin the skills transfer

SECURITY & ACCESS

  • Members sign in anonymously; the coordinator signs in with Google.
  • Firestore security rules let members read only their own data, and only the coordinator can create matches.
